When reviewing a mouse pad, accurately describing its properties is crucial for your audience. Here's what to focus on:

---
## 1. Surface Texture
- **Abrasive** / **Medium-Abrasive** / **Smooth**
- Check if the surface grips or resists materials
- Note how it interacts with skin and clothing fabrics ![[k83_texture.png]]

## 2. Friction Properties
**Dynamic Friction:**
- Resistance during mouse movement
- Lower value = smoother glide after initial movement

**Static Friction (Stopping Power):**
- Resistance to initial movement
- Higher value = better control for precise adjustments
![[mousepad_friciton_test.png]]

## 3. Glide Characteristics
- **Mudpad effect**: High static + low dynamic friction = sticky start but smooth glide
- **Consistent glide**: Minimal difference between static and dynamic friction
- Describe the overall feel when making both small and large movements
![[old_mousepad.png]]
## 4. Speed Rating
- **Control**: High resistance, precision-oriented
- **Balanced**: Moderate resistance, versatile use
- **Speed**: Low resistance, fast movements

## 5. X/Y Consistency
- Check if vertical and horizontal movements feel different
- High-quality pads maintain consistent resistance in all directions
- Note any noticeable differences in axis movement

## 6. Durability & Maintenance
**Moisture Resistance:**
- How it handles sweat and spills
- Stain resistance and cleaning ease

**Long-term Performance:**
- How quickly it shows wear
- Resistance to fraying or surface degradation

## 7. Base Construction
**Firmness Level:**
- **X-Soft**: Significant squish, allows deep presses
- **Soft**: Noticeable give, comfortable for long sessions
- **Mid**: Firm support, minimal compression

## 8. Surface Composition
- Weave type (tight, hybrid, Cordura, textured)
- Material composition and stitching quality
- Base material and slip resistance

## 9. Comparative Analysis
- Compare to popular models (Artisan, Zowie, Logitech, etc.)
- Note similarities in:
  - Speed and glide characteristics
  - Texture and surface feel
  - Overall performance profile
- Example: "Similar to Artisan Zero but with faster glide" or "Feels like G-SR-SE with better moisture resistance"

> [!info] Tips:
> 1. Test with different mouse skates for comprehensive analysis
> 2. Consider both gaming and general use scenarios
> 3. Note any break-in period requirements
> 4. Mention ideal use cases (Arena or angle shooters. Better for Tracking-Switching-Clicking or universal)
