# Description of each
## [LINEARS]
* **Outemu Silent White** - The simplest quiet switch, a little sandy, but for the first generation it will do if you have an ultra low-budget build.
* **Outemu Silver** - Standard slag, fast, with short stroke.
* **Outemu Black** - Standard slag, weighted spring.
* **Outemu Red** - Standard slag, basic.
* **Outemu Silent Peach V1** - It was ok, but now there is something better. Quiet, soft.
* **Outemu Silent Peach V2** - Good ultra-budget quiet switch.
* **Outemu Cold Plum** - Winter collection. Full nylon body, short stroke, heavy spring. Thick thocky sound.
* **Outemu Pink Lotus** - Summer collection. Lightweight, short stroke, smooth.
* **Outemu Milk Peach** - Budget regular switch, nice sound, but Cream and Jade are better.
* **Outemu Bai Lan** - Average.
* **Outemu Hanny** - Long-pole stem. Loud clacky switch (lego movie sound).
* **Outemu Lanny** - Similar to Hanny, but a little more balanced and ordinary.
* **Outemu Silent Ocean** - Not a bad quiet switch.
* **Outemu Crystal Linear** - Completely made of polycarbonate. Dry and rattling.
* **Outemu Dustproof Silver** - Slightly better than regular silver, but still so-so.
* **Outemu Cream Pink** - Well lubricated, buttery action, deep sound (but Jade is better).
* **Outemu Jade Pink** - A cooler version of Cream Pink, with a faster spring.

------------------------------
## [TACTILES]
* **Outemu Brown** - Absolute crap. There is no tactility, it feels like a dirty sand ruler with a ringing spring.
* **Outemu Orange** - A little heavier than Brown, but the same dry trash with a ringing sound.
* **Outemu Purple** - Old heavy tactile. The bump is sharp and unpleasant, the spring rings harshly on return.
* **Outemu Red Panda** - Passable clone of Holy Panda. A rounded bump at the start, but without manual lubrication it’s a so-so idea.
* **Outemu Maple Leaf** - Not a bad ringing tactile. The bump is clear, feels fast, and has a clacky sound.
* **Outemu Milk Tea** - Budget average. Due to the milky casing, the sound is a little softer, but the tactility is weak.
* **Outemu Silent Jade Yellow** - Top silent tactile device. A clear bump at the start, not wadded at all, lubricated normally. Close to Gazzew Boba u4, but softer.
* **Outemu Cream Yellow** - Tactile analogue of Cream Pink. Good lubrication, soft dull sound, pleasant running. Also close to u4.
* **Outemu Silent Tom** - Old silent tactile. The hillock is indistinct and blurred, no longer relevant.
* **Outemu Silent Grey** - Ancient cotton shit. There is almost no bump, when pressed there is a complete feeling of rubber.
* **Outemu Silent Lemon V1** - Was a popular quiet switch, but is very sandy.
* **Outemu Silent Lemon V2** - Work on bugs V1. It has become much smoother, normal quiet tactile.


------------------------------
## [CLICKIES]

* **Outemu Blue**, **Outemu Green**, **Outemu Gold**, **Outemu Ocean Clicky**, **Outemu Spring Breeze**, **Outemu Milk Blue**, **Outemu Jerry Clicky**, **Outemu Cream Blue**, **Outemu Jade Blue** - Total shit without exception. Due to the cheap design of the Click Jacket, everything inside is loose and rattling. A nasty plastic crunch instead of a normal click.
